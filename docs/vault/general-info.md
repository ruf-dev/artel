# What Is a Vault?

A **vault** is your own private notebook in the cloud. It's built on [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/), a
popular note-taking app that lets you write notes and link them together, so your ideas stay organized
and easy to find again.

Normally, if you want the same set of Obsidian notes on your phone, your laptop, and your tablet — all
staying perfectly up to date with each other — you'd need to set up and maintain your own private server.
That's a technical, fiddly job most people never want to touch.

Artel does that job for you. Click one button, and you get a fully working, private notebook that's
ready to sync across every device you own.

## Why It Matters

- **It's private.** Your vault is yours alone. Nobody else can see or touch it unless you decide to
  share it, and sharing it never means handing out your password.
- **It's always in sync.** Write a note on your phone at the bus stop, and it's already on your laptop
  by the time you sit down. No exporting files, no emailing things to yourself, no "which version is the
  latest" confusion.
- **It's backed up.** Your notes live safely in the cloud, not just on one device that could be lost,
  broken, or stolen.
- **It's easy to set up.** You don't need to know anything about servers. Creating a vault takes a few
  seconds, and a guided **Fast Setup** helper walks you through connecting it to the Obsidian app on your
  devices (see [detailed-configuration.md](detailed-configuration.md)).

## Who It's For

**The individual note-taker.** Someone who journals, plans projects, or keeps a personal wiki in
Obsidian, and wants their notes safely backed up and available on every device — without becoming their
own IT department.

**The team.** A small team or project group that wants one shared notebook everyone can write to,
without emailing documents back and forth or handing around a single shared login. Each teammate gets
their own private access into the same shared vault.

## Beyond the Vault

Once you have a vault, it can also become the foundation for other things — like letting an AI
assistant read and write your notes, or connecting other tools such as email and project trackers into
the same workspace. Those are more advanced, optional features and are covered in their own separate
guides. This set of documents only covers the vault itself: creating it, syncing it, and sharing it.
