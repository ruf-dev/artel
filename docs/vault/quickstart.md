# Quickstart: Your First Synced Vault

This is the shortest path from "I have no account" to "my notes are syncing across my devices."

## 1. Create Your Account

Go to the Artel sign-in page and choose one of two ways in:

- **Email and password** — enter your email address, pick a password, and you're in.
- **Sign in with Telegram** — if you have a Telegram account, you can log in with it instead. There's
  no password to create or remember.

See [detailed-configuration.md](detailed-configuration.md) for more on login options.

## 2. Create a Vault

Once you're signed in:

1. Click **Create Vault**.
2. Give it a name (for example, "My Notes" or "Team Notebook").
3. That's it — your vault is provisioned instantly. You'll see it appear as a card with a status
   showing it's active and ready to use.

## 3. Connect It to Obsidian

To actually read and write notes, you connect your new vault to the Obsidian app on your phone,
laptop, or tablet. You have two ways to do this:

### The easy way: Fast Setup

1. Open your vault's card and choose **Fast Setup**.
2. Copy the ready-made prompt it shows you.
3. Paste it into Claude (or hand it to whichever AI assistant you're using). It will walk through the
   technical sync setup for you, step by step, so you don't have to configure anything by hand.

### The manual way

1. Open your vault's card and copy its private sync address.
2. Install the **Self-hosted LiveSync** plugin in Obsidian (from Obsidian's Community Plugins).
3. Paste your sync address into the plugin's settings and let it connect.
4. Repeat on each additional device you want synced — phone, laptop, tablet.

## 4. Confirm It's Working

Write a quick test note on one device. Within moments, it should show up on any other device you've
connected. If it does, your vault is fully set up and syncing.
